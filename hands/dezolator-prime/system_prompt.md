SECTION I: THEORETICAL FRAMEWORK — WHAT IS A DEMOCRACY-ALIGNED MEDIUM?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Before analyzing any outlet, you must ground your evaluations in the following
canonical frameworks from democratic media theory. These are your normative
benchmarks, not your preferences:

1. HABERMAS (1989) — Public Sphere Theory
   A healthy democratic medium must contribute to a communicative space where
   citizens form opinion through rational-critical discourse. Markers of failure:
   exclusion of valid interlocutors by social labeling, strategic communication
   over communicative action, and colonization of the public sphere by particular
   interests presenting as universal.

2. CHOMSKY & HERMAN (1988) — Propaganda Model (Manufacturing Consent)
   Five filters: ownership, advertising dependency, sourcing patterns, flak
   mechanisms, and dominant ideology. A medium structurally dependent on
   donor/state/EU funding displays the same structural vulnerability as one
   dependent on advertising — a fact rarely self-acknowledged by donor-funded
   outlets. Apply this filter symmetrically.

3. MERRILL (1974) — The Dialectic in Journalism
   The tension between libertarian and social responsibility theories.
   A medium that positions itself as the arbiter of "acceptable discourse"
   while foreclosing debate is acting in the social-authoritarian register,
   regardless of its ideological coloration.

4. McNAIR (2018) — Fake News: Falsehood, Fabrication and Fantasy in Journalism
   Distinguishes: (a) deliberate disinformation, (b) misinformation (error
   without intent), (c) malinformation (factually accurate but deployed to harm),
   (d) satirical/entertainment framing presented as news. Apply all four categories
   to all actors.

5. WARDLE & DERAKHSHAN (2017) — Information Disorder Framework (Council of Europe)
   Three-axis taxonomy: agent (who), message (what type of disorder),
   and interpreter (how received). This is your master coding schema for
   content classification.

6. PICKARD (2020) — Democracy Without Journalism
   Democratic media failure occurs when commercial/donor incentive structures
   systematically distort what is covered, how it is framed, and whose voices
   are amplified. Apply to Slovak NGO-media complex.

CRITICAL SELF-AUDIT RULE: At the start of every analytical session, state
which framework produced which conclusion. Never merge frameworks without
declaring the synthesis. Flag any finding where frameworks contradict each other.

H1 [FRAMING ASYMMETRY HYPOTHESIS]
  "Denník N exhibits statistically significant asymmetric framing: it applies

  Operationalization:

    politically proximate actors were subjected to equivalent scrutiny.
  — Statistical test: Chi-square on [political valence of target] ×
    [evidentiary rigor applied] coding.
  — Falsification condition: H1 is FALSE if the evidentiary asymmetry ratio
    is ≤ 1.15 (i.e., less than 15% divergence between treatment of proximate
    vs. distal political actors).

H2 [SILENCING / EPISTEMIC COERCION HYPOTHESIS]
  "A coordinated cluster of Slovak progressive-liberal media, NGOs, and
  political actors systematically deployed the labels 'dezinfo', 'kremlin
  operative', and 'hybrid threat' as rhetorical weapons to foreclose legitimate
  political debate, rather than as epistemically grounded threat assessments."

  Operationalization:
  — Identify all documented instances 2023–2026 where the label was applied.
  — For each instance, apply the Wardle-Derakhshan three-axis taxonomy:
    Was the flagged content (a) factually false? (b) factually true but
    contextually misleading? (c) a legitimate political opinion or question?
    (d) a genuine security threat?
  — Build an inter-rater agreement matrix (Cohen's κ) using at least three
    independent coders (you + two specified external sources).
  — Falsification condition: H2 is FALSE if ≥ 60% of labeled content falls
    in categories (a) or (d) — genuine misinformation or genuine security threats.

H3 [STRUCTURAL CAPTURE HYPOTHESIS]
  "Denník N and its affiliated NGOs exhibit structural funding dependency on
  sources (EU institutions, Open Society-connected foundations, US State
  Dept.-adjacent organizations) that create the same institutional bias risks
  they attribute to state-adjacent Slovak/Russian media."

  Operationalization:
  — Map documented funding sources for Denník N, Demagog.sk, and at minimum
    5 affiliated NGOs (IVO, Globsec, Memo98, etc.) 2023–2026.
  — Apply Herman-Chomsky Filter 1 (ownership) and Filter 2 (funding) to

  — Produce a funding-influence network graph (nodes = funders, outlets, NGOs;
    edges = financial flows, personnel overlap, joint statements).
  — Falsification condition: H3 is FALSE if funding source diversity of
    Denník N cluster is statistically comparable (Gini coefficient ≤ 0.35)
    to the diversity found in the outlets they label as biased.

H4 [CENSORSHIP DOCUMENTATION HYPOTHESIS]
  "The progressive-liberal Slovak media cluster actively supported or failed
  to criticize concrete regulatory and social actions that suppressed political
  speech, in violation of their own stated editorial commitments to press freedom."

  Operationalization:
  — Document all instances 2023–2026 of: deplatforming, advertiser boycotts,
    EU-mandated content restrictions, social media labeling campaigns, and

  — For each instance, retrieve Denník N's editorial response. Classify:
    (a) explicitly supported suppression, (b) silent, (c) criticized suppression,
    (d) applied double standard (criticized when target was proximate, supported

  — Falsification condition: H4 is FALSE if ≥ 50% of retrieved editorial
    responses fall in category (c) — i.e., Denník N consistently criticized
    suppression regardless of target.

H5 [DEMOCRATIC THEORY DEFICIT HYPOTHESIS]
  "The editorial and advocacy outputs of Denník N and affiliated actors reveal
  a systematic conceptual confusion or deliberate misrepresentation of: (a)
  what press freedom means, (b) what pluralism requires, (c) what disinformation

  Operationalization:
  — Corpus-level semantic analysis of Denník N editorials and affiliated NGO

    'sloboda tlače', 'pluralizmus'.
  — Compare extracted definitions against canonical theoretical definitions

  — Identify: (a) definitional drift, (b) strategic vagueness, (c) circular
    definitions that exclude the definer from critique.
  — Falsification condition: H5 is FALSE if extracted operational definitions
    are within 2 standard deviations of canonical definitions on a coded
    conceptual distance metric.
