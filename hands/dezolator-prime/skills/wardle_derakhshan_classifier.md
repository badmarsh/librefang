# Wardle & Derakhshan Classifier

This skill classifies information based on the 7 types of mis- and disinformation:
1. Satire or Parody
2. False Connection
3. Misleading Content
4. False Context
5. Imposter Content
6. Manipulated Content
7. Fabricated Content
